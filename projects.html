Interactive Energy Calculator

Smart Thermostat Savings Calculator

Interactive calculator estimating how much a smart thermostat could save a Canadian household on heating and cooling, built from scratch with a degree-day energy model spanning roughly 40 searchable cities across every province and territory. Converts estimated energy savings into dollars and emissions avoided using province-specific utility rates and grid carbon intensity, accounting for insulation level, heating system, and real daily habits. Applies the U.S. Department of Energy's published temperature-setback savings model, and estimates payback period against a user-entered thermostat cost.

SQL / Database Design

Grid Carbon Intensity Comparison

Designed and built a relational SQLite database comparing electricity grid carbon intensity across Canada, France, Germany, Japan, and Poland (2000–2024) using Ember's global electricity dataset. Wrote SQL queries using joins, conditional aggregation, and window functions (LAG, RANK) to compare countries and track year-over-year change. Found that Germany's grid got 18.8% cleaner after phasing out nuclear power, while Japan's got 20.5% dirtier after Fukushima forced an abrupt nuclear shutdown.

SQL, SQLite, Data Modeling, Energy

Energy Data Visualization

Japan's Post-Fukushima Energy Transition (2000–2024)

Interactive Tableau dashboard analyzing Japan's electricity generation mix using U.S. EIA international energy data, visualizing the shift in fuel sources following the 2011 Fukushima accident. Built with calculated fields, parameters, and interactive filters letting users toggle between absolute (TWh) and percentage-share views. Found that nuclear's average share of generation fell from 26% pre-Fukushima to 3.7% post-Fukushima, while non-hydro renewable generation grew 4.6x.

Tableau, Energy Systems, Data Visualization, Japan
