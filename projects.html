Energy Analysis · Feb 2026

Heat Pump vs. Gas Furnace Feasibility Study

Python-based financial and environmental analysis comparing 15-year total cost of ownership between air-source heat pumps and natural gas furnaces for Ontario homes. Modeled energy consumption using Ontario energy rates and government rebates, finding a $9,500 cost difference and 94.5% emissions reduction in favor of heat pumps. Includes an interactive website showcasing results.
